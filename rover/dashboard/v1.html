🌱Recommended Organic Soil Amendment Guidelines (UP Package of Practices)
Well-Rotted Farmyard Manure (FYM)
Dose: 10 – 15 tonnes/ha
Timing: 3-4 weeks before sowing during primary tillage.
Impact: Improves organic carbon, water retention, and microbial buffering against extreme pH.
Vermicompost (केंचुआ खाद)
Dose: 2.5 – 5.0 tonnes/ha
Timing: Basal application during final seedbed preparation.
Impact: Supplies readily available macro & micro nutrients with humic substances.
Green Manuring (Dhaincha / ढैंचा)
Dose: Incorporate 45-day standing crop.
Timing: Prior to Kharif rice transplanting.
Impact: Adds 60-80 kg N/ha, lowers pH in sodic Usar soils, and improves soil structure.

💧 Measurement-Based Irrigation Intelligence

Context-aware irrigation recommendations based on measured soil moisture, target crop water demands, growth stages, and water source conveyance.

ℹ️System Meteorological Limitations & Irrigation Safeguards

No External Weather Forecast API: Agro-Bot currently operates without external satellite or synoptic weather API feeds. The onboard BMP180 barometric sensor provides localized pressure trends only and cannot substitute for verified meteorological rainfall forecasts.
Measurement-Driven: Irrigation advice reflects physically measured volumetric soil moisture (%) at probe insertion depth. Adjust irrigation timing if seasonal rainfall is visibly imminent.
